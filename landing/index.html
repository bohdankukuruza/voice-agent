An AI pharmacist who actually answers the phone.

Real phone number, real-time voice AI. Ask about a medication, place an order by spelling out your name, or check an order status — out loud, like a normal phone call.

+353 1 265 6770 Tap to call from your phone · free · capped at 60 seconds

What to try when you call

Ask "What is ibuprofen?" — get a description, price, and quantity
Order "I'd like to order aspirin" — then spell your name and confirm a quantity
Check "What's the status of order 1?" — look up an order you just placed
Interrupt Talk over the agent mid-sentence — it stops and listens, like a real conversation

How it works

01

You speak

Twilio streams your call audio to the server the instant you're connected.

02

It understands

Deepgram's Voice Agent transcribes your speech and GPT-4o-mini decides how to respond.

03

It acts & answers

Drug lookups and orders hit a live Postgres database, then the reply is spoken straight back.

This is a portfolio demo, not a real pharmacy. No medication is ever dispensed or shipped — "orders" only exist in a demo database. Please don't share real medical information when you call.
